3. **Add a school-document search tool.** Lets the assistant answer fees, deadlines, grades, and policies from the school document (R01 to R05). Priority: high.

5. **Write a sentence for each answer.** Turn raw tool results into a short answer that says where the information came from. Priority: medium.
