Riverside College Student Information (sample document for testing)
All names and figures are fictional.

Admissions
- Applicants must hold a secondary school certificate with at least five credits, including English and Mathematics.
- Applications open on 3 January and close on 28 February each year.
- The application fee is 5,000 naira. It is paid online and is non-refundable.
- Successful applicants confirm admission by paying the acceptance fee of 50,000 naira by 31 July.

Fees (per session)
- Tuition for Year 1 students is 350,000 naira.
- Tuition for Year 2 to Year 4 students is 380,000 naira.
- Payment after 30 September attracts a late penalty of 10,000 naira.
- Students may pay in two instalments: 60 percent by 30 September, and 40 percent by 31 January.

Deadlines
- Course registration for the first semester closes on 15 October.
- Course registration for the second semester closes on 15 March.
- The last day to drop a course without penalty is four weeks after the semester starts.
- Final-year project topic submission closes on 30 September.

Results
- Results are released through the student portal within 14 days after the last examination.
- A student may apply for a result review within 7 days of release, at a fee of 2,000 naira per course.
- Grade scale: A (70 to 100), B (60 to 69), C (50 to 59), D (45 to 49), E (40 to 44), F (below 40).

Policies
- A student must attend at least 75 percent of lectures in a course to sit its final examination.
- AI tools may be used for assessments only where the lecturer allows it, and any AI-generated content must be cited.
- Students must not share their portal login details with anyone.
- Academic misconduct, including plagiarism, is reported to the Dean of Students.
